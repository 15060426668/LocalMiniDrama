# Motion Ecology Counterpart Atlas

Runtime version: `1.1 / 2026-07-10`

Use this atlas when a storyboard needs living multi-layer motion rather than a static composition: active protagonists, listeners, NPC routines, prop transfer, machinery cycles, weather, water, plants, cloth, light, focus, sound-triggered reactions, consequences and tail-state continuity.

## Retrieval Rule

1. Fingerprint the shot by the layer that carries new information.
2. Retrieve one primary mechanism and at most one supporting mechanism.
3. Transfer only motion hierarchy, causal timing, spatial relation, sound logic and residual state.
4. Do not copy plot, dialogue, character identity or signature production design.
5. In a 2-4 second beat, keep one primary motion, one or two reactive layers, one ambient force family, one camera/focus operation and one sound landing.

| ME-J06 | `The Silence of the Lambs` 越狱叙事误导 | 场内人物和镜头把注意力放在显眼受害/出口，真正身份或路线藏在低优先级层，后续小证据改写判断 | 焦点和遮挡可以参与误导，但必须留下公平可回看的证据 | urgent responders focus on the dominant figure, covered secondary body remains soft in frame, one hand detail enters focus later and redirects the audience's inference | 不沉淀血腥伪装细节；误导必须公平可回看 |
| ME-J07 | `Oldboy` 真相揭露后的身体下降 | 人物从站立到屈身、跪坐或贴近地面，静止对手保持高度，机位按关键阶段下降 | 身体高度和镜头高度可把权力坍塌物理化 | standing posture loses rigidity, knees bend, body lowers toward the floor, opposing figure remains seated and stable, camera descends in controlled stages | 机位下降不是尊严公式，只在高度就是主叙事轴时使用 |
| ME-J08 | `Gone Girl` 媒体门前到室内 | 门外是公开舞台，门内是私密权力空间，跨过门槛后声音、表情、距离和所有权切换 | 同一人物跨边界可切换表演层，但尾帧痕迹应被带入室内 | crowd flashes and applause remain outside, couple crosses the doorway, door closes, public smile releases, transferred stain and tightened grip remain visible inside | 不让关门后服装、呼吸和接触痕迹复位 |
| ME-J09 | `Mulholland Drive` diner墙角 | 日常空间逐步压缩到墙角边界，镜头和人物一起接近，看见范围在转角前持续受限 | 墙、门框和转角可作为信息许可边界 | slow approach narrows the visible alley, wall edge occupies more of frame, footsteps shorten, camera crosses the corner only after the character commits | 不用无来源白闪或突然换景 |
| ME-J10 | `The Others` 门与楼板声源 | 门、楼板或天花板分隔可见与不可见空间，声音先改变人物站位，再决定是否跨越 | 建筑边界可把画外声转成可测路线和选择 | muffled sound travels above the room, listener stops beneath the path, eyes map the ceiling line, hand reaches the door latch, camera holds the unopened threshold | 不让声源在多个方向随机漂移 |

## Compression Rules

- Use the atlas for retrieval, not for copying entire scenes.
- A normal short-drama shot uses one primary atlas mechanism and one supporting mechanism at most.
- Merge mechanisms only when they share the same force source, spatial axis or information trigger.
- If two mechanisms introduce two primary actions, split the shot or schedule them sequentially.
- Preserve source story facts. Do not invent NPCs, weather, machinery, plants or props solely to make the frame busy.
- Keep information permission explicit: who sees, hears, knows, performs ignorance or receives the cue late.
- Normalize supplied scene descriptions through the source-reliability gate before attaching exact details to a named film.
