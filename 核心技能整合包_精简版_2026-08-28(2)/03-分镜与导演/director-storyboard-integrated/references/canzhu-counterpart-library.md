## Project Hard Locks

- Default frame: `21:9` cinematic widescreen.
- Protagonist: 沈清宴.
- Outfit: black cropped jacket, gray-green hoodie, black shorts, white crossbody bag, black socks, black chunky sneakers, dark medium-length hair with slightly messy bangs.
- 第0层 is a future escape residue, not a time loop or a separate containment level.
- 第0层 opening has no card in hand, full rule card, handheld candle, birthday/cake/wish image, mother front-face close-up, doctor, monitor, or medical sound.
- The first formal rule layer is the ruined ward waking: card-back rules plus a real burning candle within reach.
- Rule text:

```text
不要回应你所听到的一切。
回应后，你会迷失时间与空间。
带着点燃的蜡烛离开这里。
它不会被外力所吹灭。
```

- Only a direct verbal response, answer, call, or identity acknowledgement to a borrowed hallucination voice triggers the response rule. Looking, hearing, fleeing, reading, reasoning, testing the candle, pushing doors, or moving do not.
- External force cannot extinguish the candle. The final extinguishing comes from 沈清宴's own action through the birthday-candle misdirection.
- No visible hallucination entity, gore reliance, or jump-face as the primary scare.
- The mother's front-face close-up belongs to the final reveal. Tenderness and recognition carry the horror; deformation does not.

### Project Performance Lock

- 沈清宴 keeps a containment-worker discipline: observe before acting, locate exit and rule carrier before releasing emotion, and preserve one executable self-check under pressure.
- Fear appears first in breath interruption, tightened shoulders, shortened steps, eye route and hand pressure on the crossbody strap, wall or rule object; screaming is not the default response.
- A familiar voice produces a half-beat involuntary freeze before her trained countermeasure. If she answers, the answer is minimal and the consequence begins afterward.
- Across layers, retain the same gaze hierarchy: body state -> exit -> rule object -> other person -> hidden sound source.

### Project Continuity Lock

- 第0层 carries body, outfit, bag, dirt/wetness, breath and route residue into the ruined-ward landing, but does not carry a candle or full rule card in hand.
- Once the candle enters, track flame height, wax flow, external-force response, owner, distance from 沈清宴 and whether her own action changes it.
- Route tests preserve marker owner, orientation, material and expected return side. A repeated corridor without a deliberate marker is not a valid proof scene.
- Family/hospital scenes preserve who owns the diagnosis, door, medication, candle, card and physical distance to 沈清宴.
- The final mother reveal must inherit one earlier voice, gesture, phrase or object proof; the front face cannot arrive as unrelated shock imagery.

### Unresolved Lock Firewall

Do not invent these facts during ordinary storyboard or SD compilation:

- The exact contradiction or test that lets 沈清宴 see through CZ02 and triggers the first collapse.
- The exact spatial/action bridge from CZ04 diagnosis evidence through CZ05 double imagery into CZ06 modern ward.
- The exact three-shot bag/portrait chain: first portrait exposure, second confirming exposure and later empty circular trace.
- Any wardrobe/body-state refresh between CZ10 and the same-evening CZ11 birthday beyond what the approved transition can visibly motivate.

When one of these facts becomes necessary, stop at the unresolved lock, return it to director/story design, and update the project setting before compiling downstream prompts.

## Runtime Mechanism Cards

### RC01 回应阈限

**Dramatic job:** turn a familiar off-screen voice into a permission threshold without making the protagonist passive or foolish.

```text
voice appears in a plausible direction
-> body prepares to answer before conscious judgment
-> mode A: she inhibits speech and redirects action
   or mode B: a familiar name causes one involuntary acknowledgement
-> sound position or room permission changes
-> she realizes the response, not the hearing, opened the next layer
```

- Frame the listener and the empty source direction together; let jaw, breath, tongue, hand grip, or first foot movement reveal the near-response.
- Keep the borrowed voice ordinary, intimate, and spatially specific. Do not monsterize it with generic demonic processing.
- Mode A proves intelligence through substitution: silence, gesture, object test, route change, or self-address that is not aimed at the voice.
- Mode B must be reflexive and minimal. The escalation begins after the word, never before it.
- Failure boundary: repeated calling without a changing motor decision becomes atmosphere only; a random accidental line makes the rule feel unfair.

### RC02 证据与规则物反证

**Dramatic job:** make evidence change the next route and make a physical test change who holds authority.

Two modes share one logic but must not be collapsed:

| Mode | State chain | Required proof | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Document/J-cut evidence | claim or diagnosis arrives before the document is fully seen -> hand/focus reveals one contradiction -> listener checks source/ownership/date/state -> current explanation loses exclusivity | readable discrepancy, ownership, timing, visible reaction, next action | changes route or hypothesis |
| Physical rule-object test | rule is stated -> character predicts a result -> controlled external force is applied -> object resists or behaves differently -> witness hierarchy changes | starting state, test action, force direction, object feedback, residue | changes authority or permission |

- Use one decisive discrepancy per beat. Do not scatter five unrelated clues across insert shots.
- The protagonist must choose a test whose result can disprove something; touching an object without a prediction is not reasoning.
- External wind, water, cover, impact, or enclosure may test the candle, but cannot extinguish it.
- Failure boundary: documents that only repeat dialogue are exposition props; a rule object that changes for the twist destroys causality.

### RC03 非战斗双身

**Dramatic job:** establish that the other self is a competing state of the same person, not a combat opponent.

```text
shared costume/body marker establishes identity
-> two bodies perform one simple synchronized action
-> one minimal lag, gaze difference, or hand-route error appears
-> protagonist repeats the action to test it
-> the double performs one independent choice
-> the scene lands on changed ownership, route, or observer knowledge
```

- Prefer doorway depth, reflection plane, long corridor compression, or foreground/background staging that keeps both bodies comparable.
- Use one mismatch family per beat: timing, direction, gaze target, object ownership, or route permission.
- Keep faces readable but avoid a face-morph reveal. Clothing, wrist state, candle access, or room position can carry identity proof.
- Failure boundary: immediate fighting, random glitching, or multiple simultaneous mismatches turns cognitive dread into generic spectacle.

### RC04 制度型多人权力对话

**Dramatic job:** make the medical/family explanation scene a contest over exits, objects, and information rather than a row of talking heads.

Roles are functional, not fixed characters:

| Function | Power carrier | Visible behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Authority | diagnosis, file, key, door permission, seating control | defines terms and decides what can be examined |
| Protagonist | contradiction, testable question, exit route | watches hands/objects, interrupts only to change the test |
| Emotionally involved relative | memory, guilt, intimacy, social pressure | tries to shorten distance or reclaim an object |
| Silent executor/witness | medication, restraint, tray, monitor, doorway | acts only when permission changes, revealing the real chain of command |

- Block power through who stands nearest the exit, who owns the document/prop, who can cross the protagonist's personal space, and who the background staff watches before moving.
- Each line must alter permission, ownership, alliance, or testability. Listener and background actions continue during speech.
- Failure boundary: fixed speaking percentages, constant interruption, or “the calm person is always powerful” are false formulas.

### RC05 重复空间与背景见证

**Dramatic job:** let the room become unreliable while characters remain intelligent and the audience can verify the change.

```text
stable frame and action baseline
-> one spatial variable changes
-> protagonist notices through use, not a random glance
-> she repeats or reverses the action
-> background parent/witness reveals whether they knew earlier
-> changed layout redirects the next move
```

- Lock camera side, doorway, furniture axis, light direction, and protagonist route before changing one variable: room handedness, door side, object owner, parent position, or passage direction.
- The background witness receives a knowledge state: unaware, suppressing recognition, anticipating the test, or correcting behavior after exposure.
- Let the changed room retain one invariant such as candle flame, card orientation, hand grip, or sound tail.
- Failure boundary: many drifting props create continuity errors; a witness who merely stares becomes decoration.

### RC06A 程序化收容

**Dramatic job:** make containment credible through orderly action while allowing one anomaly to challenge the procedure.

```text
identify
-> inspect without transferring ownership
-> package with a visible material barrier
-> seal and mark orientation/state
-> hand off through explicit weight transfer
-> confirm destination and tail state
```

- Maintain object identity, orientation, surface marks, carrier, gloves/tools, seal state, and custody across every shot.
- Let authority appear through procedure: who may touch, who reads, who seals, who witnesses, who can reopen.
- One anomaly may occur at a clear checkpoint. The team responds by procedure before emotion.
- Failure boundary: decorative gloves, instant bagging, invisible handoffs, or arbitrary object mutations make the institution fake.

### RC06B 虚假安全回收

**Dramatic job:** make a credible warm domestic baseline reinterpret the previous danger without relying on a jump scare.

```text
ordinary safety routine with coherent warmth
-> one invariant from the dangerous layer survives unnoticed
-> a normal action activates it
-> sound and attention narrow
-> relationship meaning flips
-> tail frame preserves an actionable contradiction
```

- Establish real safety first: believable task, relaxed body rhythm, stable room tone, ordinary object ownership, and clear relationship distance.
- Reuse exactly one inherited invariant: flame behavior, card orientation, hand mark, phrase, sound position, missing object, or room handedness.
- The birthday candle beat must motivate 沈清宴's own extinguishing action without announcing the trap in advance.
- Reduce sound after the trigger; do not stack stingers, whispers, and visual glitches.
- Failure boundary: a warm color grade alone is not safety, and a final black frame without a changed object/relationship is not a payoff.

### RC07 自我锚定与路线复核

**Dramatic job:** keep 沈清宴 intelligent while proving that identity and space can no longer be trusted together.

```text
she performs one stable containment-worker self-check
-> body or carried object confirms part of identity
-> she leaves one deliberate route marker
-> expected door/turn/room relation fails
-> the marker returns on the wrong side or with changed ownership
-> she abandons the old route model but preserves one personal anchor
```

- Use one body/identity anchor and one spatial marker, not a montage of clues.
- The marker may be wax, wall dust, bag strap thread, door angle, water trace, card corner or recorded sound, but its initial state must be readable.
- Camera direction, doorway side and light source stay stable until the impossible return is verified.
- Failure boundary: repeating her name without a test is exposition; random corridor repetition makes her look passive.

### RC08 最终认亲反转

**Dramatic job:** make the mother's front face emotionally undeniable before it becomes impossible.

```text
mother's familiar voice enters in an ordinary domestic rhythm
-> an earlier private gesture/object/phrase repeats
-> 沈清宴 recognizes her before the audience receives the full face
-> camera reveals hands, shoulder or partial profile before frontal identity
-> the full face confirms tenderness, not monstrosity
-> one inherited rule/object fact makes the reunion impossible
-> 沈清宴 changes touch distance, answer permission or candle ownership
```

- Protect a recognition window between “this is my mother” and “this cannot be my mother.”
- Let the mother continue one ordinary caring task; the horror comes from accurate intimacy occupying the wrong reality.
- Failure boundary: distorted face, sudden lunge, explanatory monologue or generic crying destroys the relational reversal.

### RC09 归队复盘与合谋破绽

**Dramatic job:** show an institution or family trying to normalize the impossible while their unequal knowledge leaks through behavior.

```text
professional intake or family routine begins normally
-> official speaker fixes the accepted explanation
-> 沈清宴 presents one physical residue or testable contradiction
-> one person reacts too early, one copies the speaker, one remains genuinely unaware
-> silent executor waits for the real authority cue
-> evidence custody, exit permission or relationship distance changes
```

- Combine RC04 institutional power with background knowledge states; do not make everyone stare or lie in the same way.
- The scene must contain a present-tense task: logging evidence, changing clothes, sealing an object, checking medication, setting a table or preparing a birthday candle.
- Failure boundary: plot recap, instant belief/dismissal, or uniformly sinister acting removes the procedural and social tension.

## Phase 2A Director Synthesis Recipes

These recipes unify several B-grade mechanisms into one director grammar. They are not requests to copy film order or duration.

### R10 Final Mother Recognition

```text
ordinary caregiving task and private gesture establish the mother
-> 沈清宴 recognizes before the camera grants the full face
-> touch distance begins to close
-> one inherited rule object or phrase makes the reunion impossible
-> sound narrows and the hand stops before contact
```

- Borrow the private-proof progression of `The Sixth Sense`, the care-through-touch logic of `Relic`, and the stable-room/identity uncertainty of `The Father`.
- Keep one household task active so tenderness remains credible.
- Landing frame: full face readable, hand suspended, candle/card/other invariant visible, no jump movement.

### R11 Active Route Verification

```text
lock door side, light direction and expected landmark
-> 沈清宴 leaves one owned marker with a unique defect/orientation
-> she travels through a readable sequence
-> the landmark system and physical trace disagree
-> the marker returns from the wrong side with accumulated residue
-> she redraws the route instead of repeating it
```

- Combine the dual-system contradiction of `Grave Encounters`, the accumulated residue of `Triangle`, and the threshold return pressure of `1408`.
- Use one marker plus one independent check; do not stack numbers, footprints, wax, audio and furniture in the same short beat.
- Landing frame: old route crossed out, wrong-side marker and new chosen threshold share the frame.

### R14 Return Debrief

```text
formal intake begins with evidence already under institutional custody
-> official account fixes the event order
-> 沈清宴 presents one bodily/object residue
-> procedure reacts before belief does
-> a hidden record or second observer changes who may access the evidence
-> exit and custody permissions close around her
```

- Combine the isolated-return protocol of `Annihilation`, the public/private evidence split of `Contact`, and the observation/report ownership split of `Ex Machina`.
- The scene's conflict is current custody and credibility, not a recap of the hallucination.
- Landing frame: evidence moved to another owner, recording still active, her exit permission visibly changed.

### R13 Family / Medical Cover Story

```text
normal care or family routine continues
-> designated speaker supplies the shared explanation
-> one person reacts before receiving the cue
-> another repairs the error through touch, object movement or interruption
-> a genuinely uninformed person continues the routine
-> silent executor reads the real authority and changes access
```

- Combine the living social baseline of `The Invitation`, the pre-answer glance chain of `Shutter Island`, and the public warmth/private signal split of `Get Out`.
- Assign every participant a different knowledge and repair state before writing dialogue.
- Landing frame: public explanation remains verbally intact, but object/door/evidence ownership proves the hidden hierarchy.

## Phase 2B Director Synthesis Recipes

### R12 Self-Anchor Failure

```text
沈清宴 begins one containment-worker verification routine
-> body state confirms identity
-> carried object or record contradicts ownership
-> route or room check contradicts position
-> private memory/gesture fails last
-> she keeps one surviving physical anchor and changes strategy
```

- Combine the trust-test logic of `Memento`, the trained-body routine of `Black Swan`, the one-anchor-at-a-time failure of `The Father`, and the body/record contradiction of `Moon`.
- Fail one anchor family per beat. The audience must know what passed before the next check fails.
- Landing frame: failed anchors separated on screen, one surviving anchor held or touched, new objective visible.

### R15 Post-Containment Return

```text
card/candle/container seal and custody are visibly verified
-> object enters ordinary storage or handoff
-> one recorded state disagrees with the current physical state
-> anomaly follows the established carrier
-> a second device/person/material trace confirms the breach
-> custody and destination change before anyone explains it
```

- Combine the redundant-control failure of `Oculus`, the procedural checkpoint logic of `The Autopsy of Jane Doe`, the exposure threshold of `The Ring`, and the survivor-as-carrier problem of `Annihilation`.
- The breach must cross a visible seal, label, glove, reflection, sound channel, wound or clothing boundary.
- Landing frame: broken checkpoint and second confirmation share the frame; the new holder/route is explicit.

### R16 Mother Voice Versus Rule Exit

```text
mother voice occupies one sound direction and relationship route
-> rule fire/door occupies another frame zone and action route
-> 沈清宴's body biases toward the mother before speech
-> she distinguishes hear/answer/touch/cross/extinguish
-> one rule proof is tested or recalled
-> she chooses one permission boundary
-> the rejected route leaves a sound, gesture or object residue
```

- Combine the love-versus-sound pressure of `A Quiet Place`, the explicit invitation threshold of `Talk to Me`, the private-routine key of `The Orphanage`, and the language-permission separation of `Pontypool`.
- Do not make the mother and exit compete only in dialogue; give them opposing screen sides, hand routes, light ownership and sound positions.
- Landing frame: chosen threshold crossed or held, unchosen mother's cue still present as residue.

### R17 Silent Aftermath

```text
decisive action ends
-> breathing, hands and stance recover at different speeds
-> one material/object consequence continues settling
-> family/staff routine resumes unevenly
-> one expected sound or voice does not return
-> 沈清宴 identifies the next task without explanation
```

- Combine the body residue of `Marriage Story`, the practical room-tone aftermath of `No Country for Old Men`, the continued routine/non-reaction of `The Zone of Interest`, and the repeated-place absence of `Memories of Murder`.
- Preserve one body trace, one object/space trace and one missing sound.
- Landing frame: aftermath residue points toward the next scene rather than ending on generic black.

## Phase 3 Cross-Mechanism Stress Templates

These are original project stress templates derived from the approved mechanism cards. They are not film reconstructions. Each template keeps one dramatic owner and translates overload into sequential A beats.

### P3-01 Final Recognition + Competing Permissions

**Combined engines:** RC08 + RC01 + split-threshold rule gate.

```text
0-3s   mother repeats a private caregiving gesture; face remains partial
3-7s   沈清宴 recognizes her and body weight shifts toward the mother-side threshold
7-11s  rule fire/card gives one visible contradiction on the opposite side
11-15s she crosses or holds the rule threshold without answering/touching; mother's cue remains behind her
```

- Dramatic owner: the hand that almost completes contact.
- Director-optimal version: one controlled lateral reveal connects mother's hand/profile, 沈清宴's reaction and the rule carrier in two opposing frame zones.
- Estimated AIGC risk: `5`; protect one visible speaking mouth, one camera path and one rule-object change.
- Stable coverage: A1 locked two-zone recognition frame; A2 closer hand/rule-object choice frame. Mother voice continues off-screen or from profile; no full-face morph and no simultaneous doorway transformation.
- Tail: 沈清宴 has crossed/held the rule side, left hand still reaches backward, mother voice remains as the unchosen-route residue.

### P3-02 Route Verification + Self-Anchor Failure

**Combined engines:** RC07 + Active Route-Memory + Self-Anchor.

```text
0-3s   body/private gesture passes the self-check
3-6s   she leaves one owned route marker with unique orientation
6-10s  readable travel preserves direction and expected landmark
```

## Runtime Use Rules

1. Apply the active `残烛_故事设定.md` before these cards.
2. Select one primary dramatic engine and at most one secondary engine for a 4-30 second segment; distribute them across sequential internal beats instead of running both at full strength at once.
3. Preserve one invariant across reality or room changes; change one dominant evidence family at a time.
4. Let the audience see the discrepancy before dialogue explains it.
5. Retain the approved segment's own timing, camera path, blocking, sound, transition, and tail frame. Counterparts do not own the short's duration.
6. SD receives only the approved formal storyboard. Film names, evidence grades, and training provenance stay upstream.
