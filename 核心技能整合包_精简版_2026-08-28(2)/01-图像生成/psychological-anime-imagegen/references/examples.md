# Prompt Patterns

Use these as structural examples. Keep source names outside the model prompt.

## 1. Ordinary group on a road

```text
Use case: static narrative illustration
Input images: Image 1 supplies the five classmates' identities and clothing; Image 2 supplies the isolated girl's identity
Primary request: five classmates have just noticed the isolated girl approaching on a narrow residential slope after school
Subjects: exactly six children; the blonde girl owns the center of the group, the isolated black-haired girl is smaller in the background and holds both backpack straps
Environment: rural residential road, dense green hedges, concrete retaining wall, utility poles, cloudy cyan sky
Composition: 16:9 wide frame, close wide-angle social perspective, classmates across foreground and midground, isolated girl visible through a gap, road depth leading toward her
Drawing language: compact child proportions, soft rounded faces, large dark irises, large simple hair silhouettes, fine slightly uneven charcoal outlines, sparse interior lines
Shading and light: soft overcast daylight, restrained one-step cel shadows, open pale face midtones
Color script: bottle-green foliage, cool grey road, cyan-white sky, simple coral, teal, yellow and lavender clothing accents
Texture: painterly clustered foliage, faint paper grain, mild animation-photography noise
Constraints: exact subject count, stable identities, ordinary natural expressions, readable backpacks and road direction
Exclusions: glossy 3D rendering, photoreal skin, heavy gradients, text, logo, watermark
```

## 2. Social enclosure

```text
Use case: static dramatic key frame
Input images: group reference for five classmates; identity reference for the isolated girl
Primary request: the group already forms a U-shaped enclosure around the isolated girl while one hand holds the top loop of her school bag and her fingers still press the slipping shoulder strap
Subjects: exactly six children, one central blonde aggressor holding the bag, one child blocking the road, three staggered witnesses, one inward black-haired victim near the concrete wall
Environment: bright ordinary after-school road with hedge, wall, utility pole and distant houses
Composition: 21:9 wide social frame, foreground shoulders and bags create an enclosure, victim lower and smaller near the right wall, clear gap showing the blocked uphill route
Drawing language: rounded child proportions, thin hand-drawn charcoal contour, simple facial construction, flat dark hair masses
Shading and light: soft daylight remains cheerful across the group, local cool grey-green wall shadow falls across the victim
Color script: natural summer greens and cyan sky, pale skin, colorful school clothes, localized brown-green pressure shadow
Texture: clean cel fills, clothing folds at grip points, faint paper grain, sparse hatching only beneath the victim's eyes
Constraints: clear bag ownership, readable hands, stable count and clothing, non-graphic physical conflict
Exclusions: dynamic video sequence, motion timeline, glossy CGI, excessive darkness, text, logo, watermark
```

## 3. Psychological close-up

```text
Use case: static emotional close-up
Input images: identity reference controls face, short black hair, clothing and eye shape; group image supplies the distant bag color
Primary request: the girl has just looked up after losing her school bag, lips closed, one hand curled beside her body, the bag reflected as a tiny bright shape in her wet eye
Subjects: one black-haired child in sharp focus; distant classmates and the raised bag remain soft background shapes
Environment: residential road reduced to muted hedge green, concrete grey and distant clothing color
Composition: 16:9 tight 85mm-like close-up, hair partly covering the eyes, face placed off-center, compressed background
Drawing language: soft face contour, large dark iris, sparse hair strands over a large black silhouette, fine irregular charcoal line
Shading and light: pale readable skin, localized hair shadow and grey-green eye-socket shadow, narrow sky reflection in the eye
Color script: muted brown-grey, olive green and dull violet shadows with one distant saturated bag accent
Texture: localized pencil hatching under the eyes and cheek, faint paper grain, soft background bloom
Constraints: stable identity, restrained expression, readable eye and hand, matte cel-rendered skin
Exclusions: tears streaming, screaming pose, photorealism, glossy rendering, text, logo, watermark
```

## 4. Human and simple mascot

```text
Use case: static surreal character scene
Input images: human identity reference; mascot shape reference; corridor palette reference
Primary request: an exhausted school-age girl stands in side profile by a classroom door while a small rounded pink mascot rests against her shoulder
Subjects: one human child and one simple mascot; the child lowers her gaze while the mascot looks forward with two dot eyes and a tiny mouth
Environment: empty school corridor with faded teal doors, dusty salmon walls, cream ceiling and medium wood floor
Composition: 16:9 side-profile medium shot, child on the left third, corridor receding behind, mascot creating a clean pink circle against the grey shirt
Drawing language: compact human proportions, fine uneven charcoal lines, simple black hair mass; mascot uses a clean rounded silhouette and minimal facial marks
Shading and light: cool side window light, restrained cel shadows, locally deeper lower eyelids
Color script: faded teal, salmon, cream and wood brown, clean coral-pink mascot accent, grey-green shadows
Texture: hand-painted corridor, faint paper grain, slight window bloom
Constraints: clear scale contrast, stable identity, simple mascot anatomy
Exclusions: plush toy texture, realistic octopus anatomy, glossy 3D, text, logo, watermark
```
