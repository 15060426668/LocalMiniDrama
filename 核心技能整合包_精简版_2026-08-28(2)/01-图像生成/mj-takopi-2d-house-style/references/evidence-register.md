## Evidence Consensus

High-confidence constants across supplied stills and local motion evidence:

1. thin charcoal/brown hand-drawn contours with small width changes;
2. sparse facial construction and soft short jaws;
3. large irises whose highlight complexity changes with emotional ownership;
4. hair built from large silhouette masses plus a few irregular edge strands;
5. pale matte skin with one restrained cel-shadow family;
6. short cheek lines, under-eye lines, or local hatching instead of global texture;
7. whole-body intent carried by shoulders, spine, hands, knees, feet, and object contact;
8. character surfaces cleaner than painterly or materially dense backgrounds;
9. ordinary daylight and warm domestic color continuing beside distress;
10. a simple high-chroma mascot with radically lower detail density than humans and environments.

## Outlier Handling

- Promotional white-background art is strongest for line, silhouette, gesture, and flat-color evidence; it is not a universal lighting reference.
- Screenshot frames are strongest for broadcast cel behavior and facial acting; capture noise and UI are not drawing features.
- Graphic hearts, stars, handwritten text, or saturated pink fields belong to an expressive lane, not the base world.
- Dense overhead rooms are background-density evidence. Do not carry equal clutter into every portrait or key art frame.
- When one image conflicts with the majority, preserve the majority baseline unless the user explicitly selects the outlier.
