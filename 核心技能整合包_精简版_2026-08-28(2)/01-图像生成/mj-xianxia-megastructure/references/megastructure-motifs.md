# Megastructure Motifs

Use this reference when the user asks for imagination, variants, style bibles, or deeper xianxia megastructure invention.

## Design Principle

Do not only enlarge a palace. Change the law of how architecture occupies sky, cloud, mountain, time, or cosmos. The best image still has readable Chinese architectural DNA, but its scale behavior feels metaphysical.

## Motif Bank

### 1. Heavenly Capitals

- `Twelve Towers Five Cities`: layered floating palace districts stacked across clouds, each roofline glowing like a constellation.
- `White Jade Capital`: a city carved from one massive jade cliff, tiny red roofs embedded inside the wall.
- `Cloud-Root Palace`: palace foundations descend like mountain roots into clouds.
- `Sky Bureaucracy`: endless terraces, stairways, gates, and archive towers arranged like a divine administrative city.

### 2. Giant Thresholds

- `Moon Gate as Horizon`: a circular gate so large it frames mountains, moon, and cloud sea.
- `Door of Dawn`: one palace door becomes a glowing vertical sunrise slit.
- `Gate Above Weather`: the lower half of the gate is buried in cloud storms.
- `Under-Eave Sky`: a roof overhang so huge it creates its own night under daylight.

### 3. Inverted and Suspended Structures

- `Inverted Roof City`: palaces hang upside down from a dark celestial ceiling.
- `Suspended Colonnade`: white jade columns hang down into cloud layers without touching ground.
- `Floating Hull Palace`: a palace underside resembles a carved ship hull or mountain belly.
- `Mirror Heaven`: upper palace reflects into cloud-water below, making orientation uncertain.

### 4. Cloud and Water Behaviors

- `Cloud Waterfall`: clouds pour off terraces like waterfalls.
- `Cloud Canal`: rivers of cloud move between palace districts.
- `Mist Curtain`: a whole city is revealed only through vertical curtains of mist.
- `Sea of Clouds as Floor`: characters walk on a thin glass/jade platform above cloud depth.

### 5. Cosmic Xianxia

- `Starfield Inside Palace`: the interior ceiling opens into a galaxy while beams and brackets remain physical.
- `Planetary Wall`: a jade wall curves like a planet horizon, with Chinese reliefs the size of mountains.
- `Celestial Archive`: scroll shelves and palace corridors extend into constellations.
- `Lunar Court`: a palace sits half-embedded in a full moon, with terraces crossing the lunar surface.

### 6. Natural Scale Anchors

- `Ancient Pine Bridge`: a pine branch spans from foreground to palace, thicker than a road.
- `Cliff Bonsai Inversion`: a bonsai-sized tree in a pot is actually mountain scale.
- `Root Palace`: roots wrap around white jade foundations, showing age and mass.
- `Hanging Flowers`: tiny bells or flowers near the camera contrast with giant walls beyond.

### 7. Human Relationship Beats

- `arrival`: one figure reaches the final stair before an impossible city.
- `audience`: small figures wait in silence below a colossal gate.
- `banishment`: a lone figure stands at the edge while the palace recedes into cloud.
- `pilgrimage`: two figures walk toward a temple that never seems nearer.
- `inspection`: officials or immortals look down over a divine city system.
- `lost scale`: the person is nearly hidden in mist, only robe color reveals them.

## Fresh Combination Recipes

Use one item from each column:

```text
Human beat + threshold + megastructure behavior + atmospheric law
```

Examples:

- Lone pilgrim + endless bridge + cloud-root palace + sunrise haze.
- Three immortals + moon gate + lunar court + cold blue mist.
- Small official + under-eave sky + suspended colonnade + starfield snowfall.
- Banishment figure + cliff balcony + planetary wall + cloud waterfall.
- Two travelers + corridor vanishing point + inverted roof city + dawn glow.

## Palette and Light Directions

- `warm sacred dawn`: white jade, lacquer red, muted gold, soft blue shadows.
- `cold immortal night`: deep blue, silver mist, scattered lantern red, star glitter.
- `storm-bright revelation`: dark clouds, sharp white light cracks, wet stone reflections.
- `sunset bureaucracy`: bronze beams, long shadows, amber fog, endless ordered columns.
- `moonlit exile`: pale robes, black roof silhouettes, quiet cloud sea, rim-lit edges.

## Anti-Cliche Notes

- Replace generic "floating temples" with a defined urban or structural system.
- Give every huge object a function: gate, archive, court, bridge, foundation, wall, canal, observatory, prison, altar.
- Let atmosphere hide part of the giant object. Scale feels larger when the full form cannot fit in frame.
- Use one small human gesture to create awe: pause, look up, hold lantern, touch railing, walk into fog.
- Avoid making the palace merely tall. Make it unreachable, horizon-spanning, weather-making, or cosmically embedded.
