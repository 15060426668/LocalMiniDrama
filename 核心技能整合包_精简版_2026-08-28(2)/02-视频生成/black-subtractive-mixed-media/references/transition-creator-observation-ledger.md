# Transition Creator Observation Ledger

## Scope and evidence

This ledger records ten samples from each of three Douyin transition creators. Selection used the highest `digg_count` among the first public profile page captured on 2026-08-01. Douyin exposed 19-21 works per account, reported `has_more=1`, and then required login. These are therefore the visible-page top ten, not a claim about each account's all-time top ten.

Creator mapping:

- `C1`: 来解决一切的名侦探 (`creator-01`)
- `C2`: 风听雨 (`creator-02`)
- `C3`: 风信子 (`creator-03`)

Each sample has its original MP4, sampled contact sheet, cut-focused contact sheet, visual timeline, and audio-onset analysis under `assets/source-videos/transition-creators/`. `Visual event` is the strongest relevant sampled image change after the setup. `Audio onset` is the nearest detected strong onset. C3-05 uses the manually verified 3.60 s fabric/light-to-meadow transition instead of the detector's opening-motion false positive.

## Cross-sample findings

- Twenty-nine of thirty selected main visual events fall within 0.00-0.34 s of a detected strong onset. C3-01 is the lone 0.66 s exception; its delay is perceptually motivated rather than random.
- A transition is strongest when it preserves at least three invariants: screen position, action phase, motion vector, silhouette/shape, light direction, prop state, gaze, or support/contact.
- Full cover is useful only as a concealment interval. The audience must see both the preparation before it and the consequence after it.
- The destination shot is not an illustration card. It needs residual body motion, environmental motion, and enough duration for the new scale and emotion to register.
- High contrast comes from `ordinary evidence -> single trigger -> transformed world`, not from stacking multiple unrelated wipes.
- Source account names, captions, and artist identities are research provenance only. Never place them in model-facing prompts.
