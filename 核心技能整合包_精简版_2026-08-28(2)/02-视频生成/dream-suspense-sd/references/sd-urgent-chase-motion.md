# SD Urgent Chase Motion

Use this file when SD / Seedance output needs panic escape, urgent chase, frantic door action, sudden look-back, low-angle running, pursuit pressure, or stronger dynamic motion.

## Positive Motion Formula

Write urgent motion as four tracks:

```text
camera force + body force + space speed proof + sound / rhythm cue
```

Example:

```text
low angle rear tracking shot close to ground, handheld impact shake, the character sprints forward with strong forefoot push-off, shoes striking wet floor and cutting fluorescent reflections, coat and bag swinging with delayed inertia, narrow corridor walls rushing backward, overhead lights pulsing across the frame, dense footsteps, clipped breathing, low heartbeat pulse, temporal coherence, stable geometry, grounded foot contact
```

For psychological horror chase, add two more tracks:

```text
background threat state + active environment response
```

Use the six-track version when the scene needs oppressive fear instead of clean action speed:

```text
camera force + body force + space speed proof + background threat state + active environment response + sound / rhythm cue
```

## Frantic Door Opening Prompt

Use this chain:

```text
POV close-up, handheld impact shake, palm slamming onto old door, fingers scrabbling across wet metal handle, first grip sliding, second grip clamping hard, wrist twisting fast, shoulder driving body weight into the door, door jolting open, door edge crossing close to lens with motion blur, cold fluorescent light flooding through the crack, breath catching, hinge shriek, temporal coherence
```

Visible anchors:

- palm impact on door.
- fingertips searching and slipping.
- wrist tendons tightening.
- shoulder force pushing through.
- door edge passing close to lens.
- camera pulled forward by body momentum.

## Sudden Look-Back Prompt

Use when a sound hits behind the subject:

```text
medium close side-rear shot, sudden body flinch, shoulders jerking upward, eyes snapping back first, head turning quickly over shoulder, hair following with delayed inertia, torso and feet staying oriented forward, rapid lateral truck / slide along the corridor wall line, camera path parallel to the wall, foreground lights streaking with motion blur, landing on the rear doorway position transformed into damp wall, rectangular doorframe stain and rusty hinge marks visible, breath catch and sound drop
```

For POV:

```text
POV snap back, fast head-turn motion, short natural motion blur, rear wall landing frame, damp plaster texture, doorframe stain and hinge rust marks visible, body breathing shaking the frame
```

## Low-Angle Panic Running Prompt

Use for floor-level running speed:

```text
low angle rear tracking shot close to ground, 24mm wide angle, handheld running vibration, the character sprinting down a narrow corridor, shoes striking wet floor in fast rhythm, forefoot push-off and knee drive visible, body leaning forward, coat hem and hair pulling backward with delayed inertia, white crossbody bag hitting the hip repeatedly, fluorescent light patches flashing across the shoes, floor reflections breaking under each step, corridor wall lines rushing backward, foreground edges streaking with motion blur, dense footfalls, clipped breathing, cloth friction, bag buckle hits, low heartbeat pulse
```

Consistency tail:

```text
temporal coherence, stable subject identity, same face and clothing in every frame, stable corridor geometry, straight wall lines, grounded foot contact, natural physics, consistent lighting, stable exposure, same color grade, continuous camera path
```

Chinese version:

```text
贴地低机位背后跟拍，24mm wide angle，handheld running vibration，人物沿狭窄走廊高速奔跑，鞋底快速砸在潮湿地面，前脚掌蹬地和膝盖前顶清晰，身体前倾，外套下摆和头发因惯性向后甩，白色斜挎包反复撞在腰侧，冷白荧光灯影一格一格扫过鞋面，地面反光被每一步切碎，走廊墙线高速后退，前景边缘带运动模糊，密集脚步声，短促喘息，衣料摩擦，包扣撞击，低频心跳脉冲。
```

## Psychological Horror Chase Prompts

### Deep-Focus Background Threat

Use when the pursuer, voice source, or wrong detail should remain visible in depth:

```text
locked wide shot, deep focus, 35mm lens, the frightened character in the midground breathing hard and scanning the wrong side of the corridor, long corridor depth fully readable, distant doorway and wall corners sharp, a calm human silhouette walking steadily in the far background, overhead fluorescent lights flickering in sequence, wet floor reflections trembling under the character's shifting feet, background doors and wall pipes staying clear, room tone thinning, distant footstep echo, low heartbeat pulse, temporal coherence, stable corridor geometry
```

### Frantic Body-Detail Burst

Use for ordinary-person panic, failed balance, and physical contact:

```text
handheld close-up burst, 85mm lens, fingers scraping across a wet metal handle, first grip slipping, wrist tendons tightening, shoulder hitting the wall, one foot misplanting on the slick floor, knee buckling for half a beat, the other hand catching the wall and smearing moisture, coat hem snapping backward, bag strap pulling the torso off balance, nail scrape, shoe skid, wall thump, broken gasp, cloth friction, grounded contact, natural inertia
```

### Active Public Chase

Use when the chase happens in a lived-in space:

```text
side-front tracking shot, 28mm wide angle, the character cuts through a crowded corridor in a zigzag path, foreground shoulders and bags crossing close to lens, one bystander flinching backward, another turning late, a dropped paper cup rolling across the floor, background people pausing, staring, or continuing their routine, overhead lights streaking across faces, the runner pushing past with visible shoulder contact, layered crowd murmur, object drop, fast footsteps, clipped breath, stable screen direction
```

### False Safety Pause

Use for the wave rhythm after a sprint:

```text
fixed hiding-angle medium close-up, 50mm lens, the character compressed behind a doorframe edge, one hand clamped over the mouth, chest rising in short controlled bursts, fingertips trembling against the lips, a narrow slice of corridor visible beside the face, distant shadow movement passing across the wall, a loose object outside slowly rolling to a stop, room tone dropping into near silence, controlled breath tremor, distant footstep passing, small metal click from the corridor, tail frame holding on the character's widened eyes
```

### Slow Pursuer Pressure

Use when threat pressure comes from inevitability:

```text
long-lens corridor depth shot, 70mm lens, the runner crossing foreground fast with heavy breathing and uneven steps, the pursuer remaining small in the far background, walking at a calm steady pace, distance visually compressed by the lens, repeating doorframes stacking between them, foreground motion blur around the runner, background figure sharp enough to read, runner breath loud near camera, pursuer sound minimal, steady low pulse, continuous screen direction
```

## Speed Boosters For SD

Add 2-4 boosters when motion feels flat:

- `foreground doorframes passing close to lens`
- `overhead lights flashing rhythmically across the frame`
- `floor reflections breaking under each footstep`
- `camera distance breathing closer and farther with footfalls`
- `motion blur concentrated on frame edges`
- `wet floor contact splashes and small dust / water particles`
- `bag buckle hitting the body in repeated rhythm`
- `coat hem whipping backward with delayed inertia`
- `hand brushing the wall for balance`
- `camera crash stop on landing frame`
- `deep background threat staying visible while the subject misses it`
- `foreground bystander flinching as the runner passes`
- `loose object rolling after being kicked`
- `brief false-safety stillness after the sprint`
- `steady distant pursuer gait contrasting with frantic running`

## Panic Sound Cues Inside A

Use sound as movement instruction:

- `clipped breathing synced to footfalls`
- `dense footsteps hitting wet floor`
- `bag buckle knocking against hip`
- `fabric scraping during sprint`
- `fluorescent buzz rising as lights pass overhead`
- `low heartbeat pulse accelerating`
- `sound drops for half a beat when the voice hits`
- `hinge shriek and door thump during threshold crossing`
- `short whoosh from rapid lateral truck`
- `distant footstep arriving before the figure is visible`
- `normal ambience thinning as the subject listens`
- `crowd sound reacting in uneven layers`
- `object rolling to a stop during a hiding pause`
- `low pulse holding steady under a calm pursuer`

## Segment Timing

For a 15-second chase prompt:

```text
0-2s: frantic contact action.
2-4s: threshold burst.
4-7s: corridor reveal / spatial threat.
7-9s: sound hit / body flinch.
9-11s: rapid look-back / lateral truck / landing frame.
11-15s: low-angle sprint with dense rhythm.
```

Keep each beat readable through body force and visible action.

## A-Block Add-On

```text

身体力：...

```

Fill each line with visible and audible facts.
