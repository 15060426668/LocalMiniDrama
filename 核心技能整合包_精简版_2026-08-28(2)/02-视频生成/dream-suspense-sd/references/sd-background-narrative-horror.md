# SD Background Narrative Horror

Use this file when SD / Seedance output must translate 背景说话, 背景叙事, 非主体叙事, 背景信息差, 主角没发现观众发现, NPC微反应, 反射恐怖, 靠道具说话, 靠场景说话, 环境讲故事, 非台词信息传递, 世界观交代, 人物关系前史, 时间流逝, 灾难预兆, 内心外化, 权力关系, 讽刺反差, or 伏笔彩蛋 into prompt language.

## Positive Prompt Formula

Write the shot as two simultaneous visible stories:

```text
camera layer + foreground normal action + background narrative carrier + audience discovery window + protagonist ignorance proof + foreground sound mask + unresolved tail-frame evidence
```

The foreground action remains visually dominant. The background information stays readable enough for the audience to discover.

For non-horror environmental storytelling, use:

```text
camera layer + foreground anchor action + natural background evidence + story function + audience inference path + realistic sound/light support + payoff anchor
```

## Prompt Patterns

### Daily Foreground + Background Approach

```text
medium-wide locked-off frame, foreground subject doing a normal task, background corridor / window / door area remains visible, a distant silhouette slowly entering the far background, subject gaze fixed downward on the task, foreground foley masking the background movement, tail frame holds the silhouette closer in the background while the subject continues the task
```

Useful details:

- `foreground subject paying at a counter, phone screen reflecting on fingers, background glass door showing a still figure outside`
- `foreground subject eating at a table, TV light on the face, background door left in a narrow crack with half a face visible inside the dark gap`
- `foreground subject brushing teeth at a mirror, running water and toothbrush foam, background bathroom doorway opening slowly in the mirror reflection`
- `foreground subject typing at a computer, blue screen light on the face, dark office corridor behind, green exit sign outlining a quiet figure`

### Reflection / Glass / Screen Information

```text
mirror / glass / phone black screen / monitor reflection included inside the composition, subject attention aimed elsewhere, reflected surface showing an extra figure or wrong spatial detail, reflection remains inside the same frame, subject action naturally interrupts their chance to notice, tail frame preserves the reflection clue
```

Useful details:

- `stainless elevator door reflection showing a figure in the rear corner while the real elevator appears empty around the subject`
- `phone screen turning black for one beat, glossy screen reflection showing a face close behind the shoulder`
- `security monitor wall showing the room from behind, one monitor containing a standing figure at the door`
- `polished wet floor reflecting a side corridor that the real hallway has not yet revealed`

### NPC Micro-Reaction Warning

```text
foreground subject crossing the frame normally, side character in midground noticing something behind the subject, side character hand stopping mid-action, eyes widening slightly, mouth opening then settling into a forced neutral expression, subject continuing forward, tail frame keeps the side character's gaze fixed past the subject
```

Useful details:

- `hotel cleaner gripping cart handle, cart wheel stops, cleaner leans back against the wall while subject walks past`
- `ticket clerk holding torn ticket stub, fingers trembling, forced smile, gaze locked over the subject's shoulder`
- `neighbor at elevator door stepping aside, eyes fixed behind the subject, elevator light reflecting on a tense face`
- `bartender overfills a glass while watching something behind the customer, liquid spreading on the bar top`

### Collective Background Behavior

```text
wide public-space frame, foreground subject moving normally, background crowd subtly parting / pausing / turning in the same direction, crowd action remains restrained and quiet, subject keeps moving with personal sound source or focused task, tail frame leaves a visible corridor opened by the crowd
```

Useful details:

- `commuters in the platform background gradually turning their heads toward the subject's path`
- `supermarket shoppers frozen in half-actions with hands still on products`
- `office workers behind glass partitions stopping keyboard movement one after another`
- `cafeteria tables becoming quiet in a wave while the subject continues carrying a tray`

### Autonomous Object / Space Clue

```text
foreground subject occupied by a task, background object changing state with a physical trace, practical light catching the changed object, foreground sound covering the small movement, tail frame holds the new object state
```

Useful details:

- `background door lock rotating slowly, latch line widening, subject stirring noodles in the foreground`
- `refrigerator door opening a narrow gap behind the subject, cold interior light spreading on the floor`
- `stairwell lights turning on floor by floor in the background, subject opening a package in the foreground`
- `closet door sliding open, hanging clothes moving with slight delayed sway, subject towel-drying hair`

### Look-Back Displacement

```text
subject briefly checks one direction, camera holds the original composition, subject returns gaze forward, background object / figure / spatial detail now present in the opposite layer, subject attention remains on the foreground task, tail frame holds the displacement
```

Useful details:

- `subject glancing back down an empty hallway, turning forward, a figure now standing ahead in the far frame`
- `subject checking a closed door behind, returning to the computer, sofa in the background now occupied`
- `subject lowering eyes to a phone after looking around, reflection behind the shoulder now changed`

### Era / Worldbuilding Background

```text
wide or medium-wide environmental frame, foreground subject walking / buying / waiting with a simple action, background signs, public screens, vehicles, uniforms, notices, packaging, and street behavior naturally establishing the time period and social condition, realistic ambient sound from broadcast / vendor / traffic / machinery, tail frame holds one memorable world-detail
```

Useful details:

- `foreground student walking home while kicking a stone, background wall slogans, old public phone booth, audio shop posters, bicycles passing through dusty warm afternoon light`
- `foreground resident scanning a health code at a gate, background stacked delivery boxes, taped community notice, disinfectant mist, empty square, loudspeaker repeating entry rules`
- `foreground worker buying breakfast, background job-loss news on a street screen, delivery rider braking hard, resumes in young people's hands, real estate discount signs`
- `foreground traveler sitting in an old train carriage, green leather seats, thermos steam, instant noodle cup, platform vendors moving outside the window`

### Relationship / Backstory Background

```text
interior frame with foreground subject doing a quiet routine action, background domestic objects revealing relationship history, one core object and one supporting physical state, warm or cold practical light preserving realism, tail frame holds the evidence object
```

Useful details:

- `foreground person placing keys at the entrance, background shoe rack with only men's shoes and a dusty child's pink slipper, cropped wedding photo on the wall`
- `foreground person cooking two dishes alone, background table set for two, one unused bowl with dust, memorial photo with fresh incense smoke`
- `foreground man speaking confidently in a cafe, background window showing his partner walking with another person, listener smile tightening`
- `foreground new girlfriend sitting on sofa, background bathroom shelf with two toothbrushes and a damp pink toothbrush, wardrobe door showing women's sleepwear`

### Time Passage / State Change

```text
same-space composition, foreground subject repeating a simple action, background object states showing elapsed time through dust, plant growth or decay, calendar layers, faded tape marks, moved furniture, worn surfaces, changed light season, tail frame holds the strongest time marker
```

Useful details:

- `dust line around a removed picture frame, sun-faded wallpaper rectangle, dead plant beside an unopened envelope stack`
- `calendar pages layered and curled, medicine bottles multiplied on the table, chair cushion collapsed from repeated sitting`
- `child height marks on a doorframe stopping at one year, newer paint covering only half the wall`

### Inner State Externalized

```text
foreground subject performing a controlled ordinary action, background room state revealing psychology, repeated notes / scratched photos / sleepless lights / untouched food / ritual arrangement, practical sound continuing normally, tail frame holds the psychological evidence
```

Useful details:

- `foreground subject calmly washing a cup, background wall covered with repeated handwritten dates, red string connecting small photos`
- `foreground subject tying shoes, background untouched meal trays stacked by the door and curtains taped shut`
- `foreground subject smiling on a call, background mirror surrounded by crossed-out self portraits and cold desk lamp`

### Power / Social Rank Background

```text
wide frame with foreground interaction, background spatial hierarchy showing power: desk size, seat height, guards, waiting lines, access doors, who sits and who stands, who speaks and who waits, restrained room tone, tail frame locks the hierarchy
```

Useful details:

- `one person seated behind a heavy desk in shadow, others standing lower in the frame, security badge door glowing behind`
- `long table composition, subject placed at the far end under a smaller light while decision makers occupy the bright center`
- `crowd waiting behind a rope line, one official walking freely through locked doors`

### Irony / Contrast Background

```text
foreground statement or official action, background visual contradiction in the same frame, slogan / banner / award / cheerful decor contrasted with real damage or failure, realistic sound continuing, tail frame holds the contradiction
```

Useful details:

- `foreground authority pushing a vendor cart, background wall slogan about civilized service, vegetables spilled under the slogan`
- `foreground school speech about reducing burden, background exam countdown screen and students writing under piles of books`
- `foreground property launch with bright balloons, background residents holding protest banners and broken wall surface`

### Foreshadowing / Payoff Seed

```text
foreground action drawing attention away, background clue placed naturally and legibly, camera stays loyal to the foreground action, later payoff anchor preserved through object / number / mark / headline / missing slot continuity
```

Useful details:

- `newspaper headline on entrance cabinet matching a later threat, foreground hand taking keys`
- `warning poster half-covered at mountain entrance, shopkeeper hand stopping for one beat`
- `missing knife slot on a kitchen block behind a calm conversation`
- `old family photo under a bed, one face scratched out, dust visible along the frame`

## Composition Anchors

Use these to keep the background clue readable:

- `foreground subject occupies one third of the frame, background clue remains inside the opposite third`
- `deep focus keeps the foreground action and background clue readable`
- `foreground task blocks the subject's gaze while the background remains open to the audience`
- `doorframe / mirror edge / glass panel creates a secondary frame around the background clue`
- `tail frame holds both subject and background clue in the same composition`
- `main subject remains visually dominant, background clue remains stable and readable`

## Sound Anchors

Use normal foreground sound to carry the shot:

- `keyboard tapping and computer fan masking the quiet background corridor`
- `running tap water and toothbrush brushing texture covering the bathroom door movement`
- `TV laughter and chopsticks against a takeout box covering the doorway change`
- `hair dryer wind masking the bed sheet shifting in the mirror`
- `elevator hum and phone typing covering the reflected figure`
- `crowd room tone thinning while the subject's earbuds continue playing soft music`

## Continuity Anchors

For consistency:

```text
foreground action remains continuous, background clue position remains stable, subject gaze direction stays away from the clue, reflection geometry stays aligned, prop state carries into the tail frame, crowd reaction remains restrained, lighting direction remains consistent, temporal coherence
```

Chinese continuity version:

```text
前景动作连续，背景信息位置稳定，主体视线保持偏离线索，反射几何对齐，道具状态延续到尾帧，群演反应克制，光源方向稳定，帧间运动连续。
```

## Source Coverage Check

When converting from a storyboard, preserve:

- foreground normal action.
- background information carrier and exact location.
- audience discovery window.
- subject ignorance proof.
- NPC/object/reflection/space behavior.
- masking sound layer.
- tail-frame evidence.
- broader narrative function: era/world, relationship/backstory, time passage, event prelude, inner state, power hierarchy, irony, or foreshadowing.
- natural placement reason and payoff anchor when present.

If the storyboard has a background narrative clue, it must appear in both A and E.
