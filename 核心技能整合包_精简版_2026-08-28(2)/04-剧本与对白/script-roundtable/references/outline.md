## Workflow

1. Validate prerequisites
2. Confirm user identity and diagnosis granularity
3. Auto-assemble reviewers and ask for confirmation
4. Reviewer rotation statements
5. Attending-doctor synthesis

## Reviewer Discipline

- Reviewers must cite stance and evidence.
- Avoid soft language like `还行`, `差不多`, `可能`.
- At least some reviewers should surface genuine problems.
- Reviewers should not collapse into consensus unless the script really earns it.
